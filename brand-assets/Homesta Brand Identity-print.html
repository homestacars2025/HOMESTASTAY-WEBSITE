The Arch.
A single, abstract gateway. It reads as a doorway, a path, a tunnel, and the soft shoulder of the letter h — all at once. One mark, carrying the entire Homesta ecosystem.

03 — Construction
Built on a 6×6 grid.
A semicircle of radius 38u rests on two pillars of 14u stroke width. Round terminals soften the silhouette to feel approachable; the proportions stay rigid enough to remain crisp at favicon size.
